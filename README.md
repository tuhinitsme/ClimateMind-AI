## 📌 Overview
**ClimateMind AI** is an intelligent, high-availability climate resilience platform designed to help communities and local authorities prepare for extreme weather events—including cyclones, riverine floods, flash floods, and severe heatwaves. By converting raw meteorological data and forecasts into location-aware, actionable safety guidance, ClimateMind AI bridges the gap between early warnings and grassroots action.

---

## 🚨 The Problem
While millions across vulnerable regions receive early disaster warnings, they frequently suffer from a lack of **clear, location-specific, and actionable guidance** on how to protect their families, livestock, and livelihoods. Generic alerts fail to provide localized evacuation routes, shelter locations, or hazard-specific preparedness steps.

---

## 💡 The Solution
ClimateMind AI ingests regional risk parameters and instantly leverages the **Google Gemini 3.8 Flash API** to generate customized, bilingual (English & Bengali) step-by-step resilience protocols tailored to any of the 64 districts in Bangladesh.

---

## ✨ Key Features
* **Hyper-Local Risk Assessment**: Evaluates vulnerability down to the district level.
* **Gemini-Powered Guidance**: Generates dynamic, streaming actionable safety checklists via **Google Gemini 3.8 Flash API**.
* **Bilingual Support**: Instant translation and context formatting in both English and Bengali.
* **Location-Aware Alerts**: Dynamic targeting for cyclones, floods, flash floods, and heatwaves.
* **Interactive Risk Visualization**: Clean dashboard built with Next.js App Router for zero latency.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack
* **AI Engine**: Google Gemini 3.8 Flash API
* **Frontend & Framework**: React, Next.js (App Router), Tailwind CSS
* **Backend & Hosting**: Vercel (Production), Google Cloud Hosting / Firebase
* **Data Layers**: GIS Datasets & Regional Telemetry
